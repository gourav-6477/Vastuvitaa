About Vastuvitaa

A boutique architectural studio based in Gurugram, creating timeless private and public spaces.

Our Philosophy

We believe great design is a dialogue—between light and material, landscape and structure, client and architect. Our practice combines the technical expertise gained from complex public commissions with the art of bespoke craftsmanship to create spaces that are both intellectually rigorous and deeply personal.

Our Expertise

Architectural Design

From concept to completion, we craft bespoke residential and commercial spaces that are both beautiful and functional.

Interior Design

Our interior solutions focus on timeless materials, custom furnishings, and a meticulous attention to detail.

Vastu Consultancy

We integrate the timeless principles of Vastu Shastra to create harmonious environments that promote well-being.
